PM Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY)

Central scheme supporting pond construction, inputs, cages, training and marketing. Subsidy levels vary by category.

Kisan Credit Card (Fisheries)

Working-capital loans for fish farmers through banks at lower interest.

State Fisheries Department

Training camps, quality seed supply and technical visits. Ask for the Fisheries Development Officer (FDO).

Fish Farmer Insurance

Cover against losses from disease, floods and other risks. Ask your bank or fisheries office.
